About me

I am a third year undergraduate student at the University of Toronto studying mathematics, philosophy, and economics. I enjoy problem solving, mathematical reasoning, teaching, as well as writing. I am particularly interested in game theory, logic, and the philosophical foundations of mathematics. (See my profile.)

I am also a teaching assistant for MAT188H1 Linear Algebra for Engineers.
