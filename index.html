About me

I am a third year undergraduate student at the University of Toronto specializing in mathematics and minoring in philosophy and economics. I enjoy problem solving, proofs, writing, as well as teaching. I am particularly interested in game theory, logic, and the philosophical foundations of mathematics. (See my profile.)

I am also a TA for MAT188H1 Linear Algebra for Engineers.
