My custom 404 error page

You can see my custom 404 error page by going to some url under this domain that does not exist, e.g. zzalbert.com/present-king-of-France/. In addition to the regular 404 error message, the page contains a philosophy quote about "existence" or "non-existence," which is randomly selected every time the page is accessed.

My logo

The favicon you see on this website is my personal logo, which I designed in 2021. I used a squirrel because my then school (CKHS, Taipei) is known to have many of them—so many that your snacks will be stolen if you leave them in the classroom without hiding them properly. Turns out these rodents also secretly run the university I go to, i.e. UofT (see this reddit post), which is pretty cool. (Just kidding. 😅 But the part about squirrels stealing snacks at my high school is real.)
